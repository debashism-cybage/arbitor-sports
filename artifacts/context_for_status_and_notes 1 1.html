🏦 payment_method dynamic · org-configured
Aspect Detail
What it is The display name of the payment method record set by the organization — e.g. "Check / Money Order", "Online Payment", "Stripe". It is not a fixed enum.
Source Taken from payment_methods.name on the last captured or voided payment linked to the registration.
Can be nil? Yes — if no payment has been captured or voided yet (e.g. registration is still pending).
